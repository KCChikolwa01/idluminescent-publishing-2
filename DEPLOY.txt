ID Luminescent Publishing - static site for Cloudflare Pages

Upload the CONTENTS of this folder (not the zip itself) as a Cloudflare Pages project.
Direct Upload is enough. No build command.

What changed in this version
- Substack-style home feed, article and note pages, golden-ratio layout, vivid colour.
- Shop buttons: 8 Kindle eBooks and 3 paperbacks (Amazon), 4 audiobooks (ElevenReader), Kobo.
- Official Kobo covers shown where they exist; a vivid typographic jacket otherwise.

Editing
- Amazon links on the home page are numbered. Once you know which title each opens,
  tell Claude which title each link opens and the numbered cards will be named.
- Newsletter: no email box, nothing stored, until MailerLite is connected and tested.
