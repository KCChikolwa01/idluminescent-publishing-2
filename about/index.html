About the author

KC Chikolwa

An author whose work explores the inner world of the human heart through psychological and spiritual reflection.

The work

The writing examines how identity, trust, love, perception, and relationships influence human experience. It stays with belonging, emotional wounds, and the conditions that shape how people understand themselves and others.

The books are reflective and thought-provoking. They are offered as reading: a record of attention, not a set of instructions.

The imprint

ID Luminescent Publishing is the publishing house. KC Chikolwa is the author. The two are named separately so a reader can tell the imprint from the writer.
