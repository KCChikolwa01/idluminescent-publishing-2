Placements, by enquiry

Businesses, authors, and organisations can ask about advertising on this site. A placement is agreed in writing. It is not offered for sale from this page.

What this page is for

ID Luminescent Publishing may, in time, carry a paid notice, a sponsored article, or another placement beside the books and the writing. Nothing of that kind is running yet, and no rate is published here.

Who may enquire

A business, an author, or an organisation whose work sits near the concerns of this house: the human heart, love, trust, identity, relationships, and the inner life.

What to include

  • The name of the person or organisation.
  • What you hope to place.
  • The dates you have in mind.
  • Do not send payment with an enquiry.

How to write

Enquiries can be sent via the contact page.
