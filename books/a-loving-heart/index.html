About this title

You know what it is to love carefully. To reach for connection while bracing for disappointment. To give until you feel empty. To hide the parts of yourself you are afraid won't be accepted. You have wondered, maybe more than once, whether love could ever feel free again. It can.

In A Loving Heart, discover the heart that is relational by design, and begin the work of true restoration of the original design of your emotional makeup — not by trying harder, but by a heart encountered by Truth that produces love within it. Imagine waking up with a heart that trusts freely. A heart that loves without calculation, gives without depletion, and receives without anxiety. A heart so connected that love does not merely flow through it, love dwells within it. Permanently. Completely.

This is the destination. And this is exactly where the journey leads. Please read this book, and know which Heart relates effortlessly.
