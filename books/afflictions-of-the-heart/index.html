About this title

Why does it often seem that no matter how good things are on the surface, there's something missing inside? Why do we feel that relationships can't deliver the security, acceptance, love, or belonging we crave? Why do we both crave connection and yet fear it? What underlies our struggles with trust, doubts about ourselves and others, our difficulties in relating, communicating, and connecting?

Afflictions of the Heart looks beneath the surface to examine the condition of the heart and how it affects our approach to life, others and ourselves.

Using psychological insight and spiritual understanding, this book explores the deeper issues behind our difficulties with relationships, sense of lack and feeling that something is missing, and the ways we reach out and connect — or not.

Ultimately what the book shows is that what we seek can only be found by looking within, and connecting to the inner source of love, not in people or things.

If you have ever struggled with feelings that things are somehow not quite right, this book will help you see what it is that you might need to mend your world. If you have a desire to know why you struggle with the relational patterns, please read this book.
