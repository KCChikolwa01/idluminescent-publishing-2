About this title

Why do we crave love, trust and belonging, yet so often find them elusive?

The Relational Heart explores the human heart and the forces that shape our ability to connect and relate to others. Through an examination of identity, empathy, trust, morality, relational law, family, community, wounds and restoration, it seeks to uncover how our relational capacities operate and how the condition of our hearts determines the ways we experience and express love.

Why do we long for connection yet struggle to engage relationally? Why do rules create order but not necessarily love? Why does a wounded heart begin to close down, protect itself or seek self-sufficiency? At the centre of these questions is trust, whose presence opens the heart to others and whose absence drives it towards protection and distrust.

Yet understanding our wounds is not enough. If love is what our hearts truly crave, what is the Source that brings them to life? If our hearts were created to know and experience Love, where can they find its Source?
