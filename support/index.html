Support

For readers and booksellers

ID Luminescent Publishing keeps support simple. Write about a title, a quotation, or a listing, and the house will answer when it can.

A listing looks wrong

If a title, series, or retailer link is missing or mistaken, write with the page and what should change. Corrections are made on the title itself.

You want to quote from a book

Permission to quote is handled by the publisher. Name the title, the passage, and where it would appear.

Booksellers and librarians

Trade enquiries use the same correspondence. ISBN and format are added to a title only when they are confirmed.

The newsletter

If you receive a message you did not ask for, use the unsubscribe link in that email. You can also manage signup on the newsletter page.

How to write

Email the house at info@idluminescent.co.za. You can also use WhatsApp and the other channels on the contact page.
